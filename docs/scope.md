# SignBridge AI — Domain Scope & Problem Statement Alignment

## Problem Statement
- **Event**: Hack 2 Ignite 2026-27
- **Problem Statement ID**: AI-01
- **Focus Area**: Accessibility-focused Indian Sign Language (ISL) communication

## Explicit Scope Definition

SignBridge AI is developed specifically as a **healthcare reception prototype**.

### In-Scope Scenarios:
1. Patient arrival and reception greeting.
2. Directing patients to departments (Registration, OPD, Emergency, Pharmacy, Billing).
3. Basic symptom and assistance inquiries ("Where is OPD?", "Need wheelchair", "Doctor available?").

### Out-of-Scope (Explicit Non-Goals):
1. General-purpose or open-domain ISL conversation translation.
2. Full medical diagnosis or complex clinical dialogue translation.
3. Unrestricted vocabulary translation outside the healthcare reception domain.

> [!NOTE]
> Framing the prototype around a targeted domain enables high sequence classification reliability, lower latency, and actionable evaluation metrics for accessibility impact.
